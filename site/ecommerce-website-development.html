Quick answer

Ecommerce development can cover product catalogues, categories, search and filtering, product pages, cart and checkout, payments, shipping, policies, analytics, order notifications, and administration. The platform should be selected after the operational requirements are clear.

Core scope

Plan the complete buying journey

Product discovery

Categories, search, filters, product information, images, variants, availability, and related products help customers make a decision.

Checkout and payments

Cart behaviour, address capture, payment provider, taxes, shipping, confirmation, and failure recovery need to work as one journey.

Store operations

Product editing, order handling, fulfilment status, refunds, notifications, promotions, and reporting affect daily ownership.

Trust and measurement

Policies, contact options, security basics, analytics, conversion events, and technical search foundations support sustainable growth.

Platform decision

Shopify, managed platform, or custom build?

Use a managed platform when

Standard catalogue, checkout, payment, and administration tools cover the business process and speed of launch is important.

Consider custom development when

Pricing, permissions, fulfilment, integrations, product configuration, or customer workflows cannot be handled safely by standard apps.
