Audit coverage

Five areas that influence enquiries

Search foundations

Titles, descriptions, headings, indexability signals, page intent, internal navigation, and visible content gaps.

Mobile experience

Readability, navigation, layout, touch targets, forms, and calls to action on smaller screens.

Offer clarity

Whether visitors can quickly understand what you provide, who it is for, where you serve, and what to do next.

Trust signals

Business identity, contact information, portfolio evidence, policies, testimonials when genuine, and ownership clarity.

Conversion path

Calls, WhatsApp, forms, page hierarchy, friction points, and the path from interest to enquiry.

Visible technical issues

Broken journeys, inconsistent metadata, obvious performance concerns, missing assets, and confusing page behaviour.

Free website audit FAQs

Is the website audit free?

Yes. The initial review of publicly accessible pages has no audit fee and does not obligate you to purchase implementation.

What will you review?

SEO foundations, mobile usability, offer clarity, trust signals, conversion paths, navigation, and visible technical issues.

Do you need website access?

No. The initial audit uses public pages. Never submit passwords, private keys, customer data, or confidential credentials.

No. It identifies opportunities; rankings and commercial outcomes depend on implementation, competition, authority, quality, and search-engine decisions.
