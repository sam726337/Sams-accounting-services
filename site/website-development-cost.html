Direct answer

Website development cost cannot be estimated responsibly from the words “business website” alone. Page count, custom design, content readiness, ecommerce, integrations, migration, accessibility, testing, deployment, hosting, maintenance, and response time all affect the quote.

Cost drivers

What changes the project price?

Pages and content

A five-page site with ready content differs from a large service catalogue that needs research, writing, photography, or migration.

Design depth

A proven layout system costs less than a fully custom visual direction with complex interactions, illustration, or many unique templates.

Features and integrations

Logins, dashboards, payments, booking, CRM, ERP, APIs, search, filters, and permissions increase development and testing effort.

Ownership and support

Hosting, monitoring, backups, updates, analytics, training, content changes, and response commitments affect ongoing cost.

Scope levels

Compare common website requirements

Starter website

Core company pages, responsive layout, enquiry action, basic analytics, metadata, and deployment.

Growth website

More service pages, stronger content structure, case studies, lead flows, integrations, and ongoing publishing needs.

Ecommerce or custom

Catalogue, checkout, data, permissions, integrations, automation, and operational workflows that require deeper discovery and testing.

Quote checklist

Information that makes an estimate accurate

Business and audience

  • Primary goal and conversion action
  • Target customers and countries
  • Existing website and brand assets
  • Competitors or reference websites

Scope and delivery

  • Required pages and features
  • Content and product-data readiness
  • Integrations and access requirements
  • Timeline and post-launch support

Questions

Website cost FAQs

How much does a website cost?

A reliable quote requires the actual scope. A page count alone does not capture design, content, features, integrations, or support.

Why does ecommerce cost more?

It adds catalogue data, cart and checkout, payments, shipping, policies, notifications, administration, and additional testing.

How can I get an accurate quote?

Provide your goal, users, pages, features, content status, integrations, reference sites, timeline, and support needs.
